// The Build Sequence

From visit to handover

Five deliberate stages — each checked and signed off before the next begins.

Site Visit & Measurement

We measure, photograph and check ground condition, power availability and automation constraints. You receive the measurement sheet, not just a verbal quote.

Week 0
Week 1

Design & Fixed Quote

CAD drawings with 3D preview, material schedule and a guaranteed fixed price contract — site conditions discovered later are our risk, not yours.

Fabrication & Finishing

Steel is cut, rolled, welded and oven-baked in-house. You receive staged photo updates and are always welcome to visit the floor unannounced.

Week 2–6
Week 6–7

Installation & Testing

Foundation prep, laser alignment, automation wiring and 50-cycle load testing — followed by a client walkthrough and a 5-year warranty card.

Warranty & Service

5-year structural and finish warranty, annual free tune-ups for the first two years, and a guaranteed 48-hour service response promise.
