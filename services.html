01

Driveway Gates

From hand-operated double leaves to 6-metre cantilever slides with GSM automation — built around your motor, not in spite of it.

  • Swing, slide and cantilever configurations
  • Automation-ready tracks, bracing and lock points
  • GSM, intercom and app-integrated access control
  • Pedestrian wicket gates built into the main leaf
  • 6–8 week lead time · fixed quote after site visit
02

Security Grills

Collapsible, fixed and ornamental grills with concealed locking — security that reads as architecture, not as a cage.

  • Collapsible grills on steel or brass channels
  • Anti-lift and anti-jimmy locking hardware
  • Emergency quick-release options for fire safety
  • Ornamental scroll and bar patterns for villas
  • Factory powder coat — never on-site paint
03

Railings & Balustrades

Stair, balcony and mezzanine railings in steel, glass-clamped or mixed material — engineered to code, built to be gripped for decades.

  • IBC & NBC compliant heights and load ratings
  • Glass, SS and timber inlay combinations
  • Frameless glass balustrades for modern homes
  • Child-safe baluster spacing as standard
  • Wall-mount, floor-mount and fascia options
04

Window Guards

Pressed and rolled bar guards for homes and towers — with concealed fixing lugs and finishes matched to your window frame.

  • Pressed-bar and rolled-bar patterns
  • Concealed lugs — no ugly face plates on the frame
  • Removable service panels where maintenance access matters
  • Powder-coated in 16 standard shades
05

Pergolas & Canopies

Garden, carport and entrance canopies in steel with louvered or polycarbonate tops — shade engineered for your sun angles, not improvised.

  • Fixed and louvered slat systems
  • Wind-load calculated for your location
  • Integrated gutter and rain-chain drainage
  • Mounting to deck, slab or between walls
06

Custom Fabrication

Sculptural screens, pivoted doors, fire-rated partitions, light-fittings and stair sculptures — if you can draw it, our studio can build it.

  • Design consultation and CAD feasibility study
  • Prototyping in mild steel before final material
  • Stainless, brass, corten and galvanised options
  • Architect drawings welcomed — we build to them

// The Build Sequence

From visit to handover

Week 0

Site Visit & Measurement

We measure, photograph and check the ground condition, power availability and automation constraints. You get the measurement sheet, not just a verbal quote.

Week 1

Design & Fixed Quote

CAD drawings with 3D preview, material schedule and a fixed price. The price is fixed by contract — site conditions we discover later are our risk, not yours.

Week 2–6

Fabrication & Finishing

Steel is cut, rolled, welded and finished in-house. You receive staged photo updates and are welcome to visit the floor unannounced.

Week 6–7

Installation & Testing

Foundation prep, erection, alignment, automation wiring and load testing — followed by a family demo and a warranty card.

Ongoing

Warranty & Service

5-year structural and finish warranty, annual free check-up for the first two years, and a 48-hour service response promise.
