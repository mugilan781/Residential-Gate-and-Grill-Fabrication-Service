// Good to Know

Questions we hear most

A standard driveway gate runs 4–6 weeks from confirmed design: 1 week for drawings, 3–4 for fabrication and finishing, then a single-day installation. Complex scrollwork or automation extends the middle stage, never the estimate.

Yes. We supply and fit automation from leading brands, sized to your gate's weight and usage. Our fabrication is automation-first: tracks, pivots and bracing are built so the motor bolts on cleanly with no retrofitting.

Every product is sand-blasted, zinc-phosphated and powder-coated in our booth — never painted on site. For coastal projects within 5 km of the sea we upgrade to galvanised or stainless grades. All fabrications carry a 5-year anti-corrosion warranty.

Absolutely. We work from architect drawings, reference photos or even a sketch on a napkin. Our studio converts your reference into CAD, flags structural issues early and confirms the quote before any steel is cut.

5 years on structure and finish against rust, weld failure and coating defects. 1 year on automation hardware. We service what we build — a call or WhatsApp gets a technician, not a voicemail loop.
