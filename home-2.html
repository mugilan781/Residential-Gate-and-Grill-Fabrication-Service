// Why Ironhaus

A foundry mindset, on a driveway scale

Every gate leaves our floor with a maker's tag — panels signed at the bench, the way workshops did it a century ago.

  • One roof, six cells Design studio, rolling, welding, finishing, automation and install — one accountable team.
