1. Who we are

IRONHAUS Fabrication Works ("IRONHAUS", "we", "us") is a bespoke gate, grill and railing workshop operating from No. 42, Peenya Industrial Area, Bengaluru 560058. We design, fabricate and install residential and light-commercial metalwork across the greater Bengaluru region.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you send via our forms, email or phone — name, email address, phone number, project address.
  • Project information you share with us — measurements, photographs, sketches, budgets and timelines.
  • Technical data — standard web logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) used for security and analytics.

3. Why we collect it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, arrange site visits, deliver fabrication and installation work, and to honour warranties and aftercare commitments. Where you have opted in, we may send occasional project inspiration by email — every message includes a one-click unsubscribe.

4. How we share it

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. Information is shared only where necessary to deliver our services: with our in-house installation crew, sub-contracted service providers (for example powder coating and powder suppliers) bound by confidentiality, and with regulators or courts when required by law.

5. Retention

Project records are kept for the period required by Indian law for business and tax documentation — typically eight years. Marketing consent records are retained until you withdraw consent. Enquiry data with no project outcome is deleted within twelve months.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time, and you may withdraw marketing consent freely. To exercise any of these rights, write to hello@ironhaus.in. We respond within thirty days.

7. Cookies

This website sets no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking. We use local storage purely for your visual preferences — the light or dark theme and the reading direction you select. You can clear these at any time from your browser settings without affecting the site.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@ironhaus.in, or by post to the workshop address above, marked "Privacy".
