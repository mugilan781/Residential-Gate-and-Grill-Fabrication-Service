18+Years of Craft

// Our Story

From a two-man shed to twenty-eight craftsmen

IRONHAUS began in 2008 with one welder, one secondhand rolling machine and a single promise: finish every gate like the founder's name is welded onto it.

That promise became a habit. Word travelled between neighbours, then between architects. We added a design studio when clients asked for custom scrollwork, a finishing booth when monsoon rust became the industry's dirty secret, and automation bays when gates started opening themselves.

Today the shed is a 12,000 sq.ft floor in Peenya, but the rule hasn't changed — nothing is drawn by one firm, welded by another and installed by a third. It never leaves our hands until it stands on your driveway.

// What We Stand On

Four rules we don't break

Honesty in Steel

The grade, the gauge, the welder's name — if it's in the quote, it's in the build. Nothing substituted at the last minute.

Fixed Quotes

The number we quote is the number you pay. Site surprises are our problem, not a change order.

Date Certainty

We quote lead times we can defend. Late installations are refunded on the penalty day, not apologized for.

Craft Before Speed

Scrolls are rolled by hand, welds are ground clean, finishes are baked. If it takes two more days, it takes two more days.

// Milestones

Eighteen years, inch by inch

The moments that shaped the workshop — small decisions that compounded.

2008

The Shed

Ravi Iyer and a hired welder take over a 400 sq.ft shed in Peenya. First commission: a driveway gate for the landlord's own house.

2012

First Automated Gate

Our first sliding-gate motor install. We learn the hard way that automation is designed in, not bolted on — a lesson that became policy.

2015

The Finishing Booth

Bengaluru's monsoon exposes everyone's shortcuts. We build an in-house sand-blast and powder-coat booth — the first in our neighbourhood.

2019

Design Studio

An architect's scribbled sketch becomes our 100th project. We hire a draughtsman, buy CAD tools and start drawing before cutting.

2023

500th Installation

Project 500 is delivered — a stainless and brass gate in Whitefield. Twenty-eight craftsmen now work across six fabrication cells.

2026

Beyond Bengaluru

Installation crews now travel across Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, while the workshop stays — deliberately — in one place.
