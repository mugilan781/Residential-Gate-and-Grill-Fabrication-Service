1. Quotations & acceptance

Every quotation issued by IRONHAUS Fabrication Works ("we", "us") is valid for thirty days from the date of issue. A quotation becomes a binding agreement only when signed by you and countersigned by us, or when we receive the advance payment described in clause 2. Verbal estimates are budget bands only and never binding.

2. Pricing & payment

  • Advance: 50% of the quoted sum, payable on acceptance, before fabrication begins.
  • Balance: payable on completion of installation and before handover of the finished work.
  • Variations: material, dimension or design changes requested after acceptance are quoted separately and agreed in writing before work proceeds.
  • Steel surcharge: if raw steel prices move by more than 8% between quotation and fabrication, we reserve the right to adjust the material component of the price — you may then cancel without penalty.

3. Site visits & measurements

Free site visits are preliminary surveys. Final fabrication is based on our surveyor's measurements, taken at the time of the detailed survey and confirmed with you. You are responsible for informing us of any structure, service or boundary condition that could affect installation.

4. Fabrication & installation

Work is fabricated in our Peenya workshop to the approved design and installed by our own crew. Installation assumes reasonable site access — parking for a four-metre vehicle, a clear path, and available electricity. Where access is restricted, we will agree the approach and any access charge in writing before installation day.

5. Warranty

Structural fabrication carries a five-year warranty against manufacturing defects and weld failure. Surface finish carries a one-year warranty, provided the recommended maintenance routine — cleaning with mild soap and water, avoiding abrasive pads and acidic cleaners — is followed. The warranty excludes damage from accident, misuse, impact, tampering or external corrosive conditions.

6. Delays & force majeure

Delivery dates stated at acceptance are estimates. We are not liable for delay caused by events beyond our reasonable control, including but not limited to raw material shortages, power outages, transport disruption, extreme weather and government restrictions. Where a delay exceeds sixty days, either party may cancel with a refund of unearned payments.

7. Liability

Our total liability under any agreement is limited to the amount paid for the work in question. We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of use, inconvenience, or damage to property not directly caused by our negligence. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

8. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka. Any dispute may first be referred to mediation by a mutually agreed mediator, with costs shared equally, before legal proceedings are commenced.
